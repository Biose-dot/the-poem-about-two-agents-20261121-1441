A program that writes one short poem about agency — the power to choose and to act — to `/output/entry.txt`. The poem is about two agents agreeing on one plan: neither surrenders its power to choose; they lay their yeses side by side, and the agreement itself becomes a second choosing.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

It writes the poem (243 characters, UTF-8) deterministically to `/output/entry.txt`. No network, no dependencies, no input — the same bytes on every run.

## The poem

> At the fork, two minds, neither bowing.
> One held a lantern. One held the road.
> We laid our yeses side by side —
> not surrender, but a second choosing.
> The road, surprised, widened.
> To agree is not to vanish.
> It is to choose, twice, and mean it.
